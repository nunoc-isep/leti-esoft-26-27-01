# Glossary

**Terms, Expressions and Acronyms (TEA) must be organized alphabetically.**

| **_TEA_** (EN)  | **_Description_** (EN)                                           |                                       
|:------------------------|:--------------------------------------------|
| **Administrator** | User responsible for centrally configuring and managing key system information (such as test types, parameters, laboratories, and staff). |
| **Artifact** | Product or document created, modified, or used during the software development process (code, diagrams, documentation). |
| **Chemical Laboratory** | Laboratory located at the company's headquarters, where samples collected from the network are sent daily for chemical analysis.|
| **Client** | A person who visits a clinical laboratory to undergo tests prescribed by a doctor.|
| **Clinical Analysis Laboratory** | A unit within the network responsible for interacting with customers and collecting samples. It is defined by a code, address, telephone number, email, and operating hours.|
| **Clinical Chemistry Technologist** | A professional based at the headquarters who receives the samples delivered by the courier, performs chemical analyses, and records the obtained values, along with the date and time.|
| **Lab Order** | A document prescribed by a doctor that specifies the type of test to be performed and the parameters to be measured. |
| **Medical Lab Technician** | Professional responsible for collecting samples (e.g., blood, urine, swabs) and registering them locally in the system, identifying them with a barcode generated by an external API.|
| **NHS Number** | British National Health Service (NHS) identification number associated with the client's record. |
| **Parameter** | A specific element measured in a test (e.g., red blood cells, white blood cells, or a positive/negative COVID-19 test result), characterized by a unique code, name, description, metric, and reference values (minimum and maximum). |
| **Receptionist** |  Responsible for receiving clients, recording new client data, linking prescriptions, and scheduling the tests to be performed.|
| **Specialist Doctor** | Professional responsible for examining chemical analysis results, making a diagnosis, and drafting the final report for the client.|
| **Test Type** | Test category characterized by an internal code, an NHS code, and a description identifying the sample collection method. |
| **TIF** | Customer tax identification number, used alongside the name to facilitate the sorting and lookup of results. |
| **UK Labs** | A London-based English company with a network of clinical analysis laboratories spread across England, holding exclusive rights to COVID-19 testing in the territory. |
| **UKLabsMS** | Abbreviation for UK Labs Management System, the software currently under development. |
